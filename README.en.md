# drag · Roadmap Editor

[繁體中文](README.md) · [Desktop downloads](https://github.com/SortingZeal/drag/releases/latest)

Open **About → Website** on the repository page to use the web test version. No installation or account is required. Choose English from the language menu in the header.

Use the Timeline, Image and Tracking tabs on the left of the header. Click a date on the timeline to choose Day, Week, Month, Quarter or Year, or drag the date strip sideways to pan. In Image, hover over a project name or task bar and drag its dotted grip to reorder it. An insertion line shows where the item will land, including in another project. Drag a task bar horizontally to reschedule it. Choose **Customize toolbar** from the menu on the right to reorder tools or add dividers and flexible space, then select **Done** to save the layout on this device.

1. Open **Documents** in the top toolbar to switch, create or manage documents. A check mark identifies the current document. Click the workspace title beside **drag** to edit it. Press Enter to save or Escape to cancel. To import JSON, open **Project actions**.
2. Select a task in the outline to choose a status, drag the progress slider or enter an exact percentage. Setting progress to 100% also marks the task as Completed. Choosing Completed sets 100%, Not started sets 0%, and In progress or Blocked clears the previous percentage. You can then enter a new report. Undo restores both values together. The outline, timeline and image show the tracking values you enter. Both views put the stage status in a badge at the upper left of each task bar. A pale dashed track marks 0%. Diagonal stripes cover the completed portion and accelerate at 30%, 50%, 70%, 80%, 90% and 95%. The filled length follows the exact percentage. At 100%, wider bands of light move to the right at regular intervals. All completed bars reveal the same underlying pattern, so the diagonals line up across rows and several can be visible in a long bar. A check mark appears where space allows. Select a project to see how many tasks have reported progress and their average. Double-click a name or press F2 to rename it. Drag a task onto another project to move it there.
3. Switch between Timeline and Picture in the header. Open the view settings for the title, date range, alignment and date marker. In either view, click a task bar to edit it, drag the bar to move its dates, or drag a milestone to reschedule it. Both views open the same properties panel beside the point you click, clear of the calendar and chart edges. Click another task to switch or tap empty space to close. Click a status badge or milestone to open its controls. Choose Start or End to set a milestone to that date. Milestone names and dates sit side by side in the picture, using another line when space is tight. The picture initially fits the window. Drag empty space to pan, or hold Shift to pan from a task.
4. Use **Export image** in the toolbar for an animated GIF, SVG or PNG. **Undo** and **Redo** are also available directly in the toolbar. Choose **Save project** under **Project actions** for an editable JSON copy. A 16:9 image exports to GIF at up to 1920 × 1080.

Both starter roadmaps include tasks with unreported, 0%, 50% and 100% progress. Drag a sample task’s progress slider to compare the stripe speeds at 50% and 95% and the completion sweep at 100% in either view. Choose Original, Subtle glow or Soft glow under Settings → Interface → Completed task highlight. Original is the default. The browser or desktop app remembers your choice for both views and exported GIFs. The preview respects the system’s reduced-motion preference and the Reduce motion option in Settings → Interface.

Select **Tracking** in the header for a dedicated weekly review page. It shows the week’s dates and the number of tasks in each category. Choose a project and filter for blocked tasks, tasks due this week, overdue tasks, past deadlines awaiting confirmation, or tasks with no reported progress. Incomplete blocked tasks appear even if their due date is far away or open. A task that is both blocked and overdue shows both reasons in one row. Select a task, update its status, completion and optional note, then choose **Save and next**.

Files and automatic backups stay in the browser or desktop app where you created them. Export each file before clearing site data or switching devices. Settings controls the interface palette, typeface, canvas background and language.

On Windows x64, launch the portable EXE. On Apple Silicon Macs, open the DMG or extract the ZIP to get the app. The release includes a manifest and SHA256 checksums. The app is ad-hoc signed and has not been notarized by Apple.
